# India Orders

## **How Made to Order Works**

Every piece of jewelry is made just for you, step by step, and you get updates along the way so you always know what's happening.

1. Confirm your details – Tell us your ring size and if you want any engraving (like a name or date).  
2. Order placed – Once you confirm, we start making your jewelry.  
3. CAD image – We send you a CAD image first. Think of it like a digital drawing or preview of your jewelry, so you can see exactly how it will look before it's actually made.  
4. CPX & casting update – Next, we share an update when your jewelry moves into casting. This is the step where the metal is shaped into your actual design.  
5. Stone setting – After casting, the stones (like moissanite or diamonds) are carefully set into the jewelry.  
6. Final images – Before we ship anything, we send you final photos of the finished piece.  
7. Your approval – You check the photos and give a thumbs up.  
8. Shipping – Once you approve, we pack it up and send it to you.

| Product | Handling Time |
| :---- | :---- |
| Ring, earring, pendant | 5-7 business days |
| Bracelet | 8-10 business days |
| Necklace | 10-15 days |
| Hip hop pendant | 10-12 days |

Note: These timelines may increase or decrease depending on your approval time or any sudden/unforeseen circumstances we will keep you informed accordingly.
