Brand Description

BRAND SUMMARY

Total Family is a legacy and family-alignment platform for families, advisors, wealth consultants, and enterprise firms. It helps families define a shared Vision, preserve stories, document values, clarify roles, track milestones, and strengthen connection across generations.

The brand should not feel like a wealth-management app, legal tool, or generic SaaS dashboard. The emotional center is legacy made practical: turning family memories, values, conversations, and long-term purpose into a guided, structured, and beautifully organized system.

Total Family should feel thoughtful, human, timeless, quietly premium, emotionally warm, advisor-trusted, and systemized. The brand lives between a family archive, a private library, and a modern guided software platform.

Mission: Help families preserve what matters, align around shared values, and build a living legacy across generations.

Vision: Become the trusted platform for families and advisors who want to make legacy work structured, meaningful, repeatable, and deeply personal.

Tone: Warm, calm, premium, reflective, human, trustworthy, and clear. Avoid hype, loud SaaS language, aggressive sales tone, and overly financial messaging.

VISUAL STYLE

Total Family’s visual style should combine heritage, emotional storytelling, and clean product clarity.

Use a quiet premium design system with warm editorial layouts, archival textures, vintage family imagery, serif typography, soft shadows, rounded UI cards, and calm dashboard compositions.

The design should feel like a premium family archive supported by elegant software. It should be emotional but not sentimental, structured but not cold, premium but not flashy.

Use:
- warm ivory and parchment backgrounds
- deep charcoal sections
- vintage paper texture
- archival family photos
- old letters, books, albums, wax seals, handwritten notes
- rounded software cards and timeline layouts
- calm profile avatars and family-member chips
- subtle rust and bronze accents
- clean advisor-facing dashboards
- editorial serif headlines with clear sans-serif UI text

Avoid:
- neon gradients
- cyber/futuristic visuals
- playful startup SaaS design
- cartoon families
- generic finance imagery
- luxury real estate visuals
- shiny boardroom stock photos
- overly perfect family portraits
- abstract AI networks
- loud colors
- cluttered UI screenshots

Color Usage Rules

Use deep charcoal and warm ivory as the foundation.
Use beige and warm grey to create archival softness.
Use rust and bronze sparingly as heritage accents, not as bright SaaS colors.
Avoid bright blue, neon purple, electric gradients, and overly modern tech palettes unless absolutely required by the creative brief.

TYPOGRAPHY

Headlines should use an elegant, high-contrast serif that feels literary, established, human, and premium.

Recommended headline font styles:
- Editorial New
- Canela
- Cormorant Garamond
- Playfair Display
- Libre Baskerville

Headline rules:
- use large, calm serif headlines
- use generous line height
- use sentence case or title case
- avoid all-caps for main headlines
- avoid tight tracking
- use italic serif only for emotional words, legacy phrases, or editorial emphasis

Body and UI text should use a clean, neutral sans-serif.

Recommended body/UI font styles:
- Inter
- Satoshi
- Neue Haas Grotesk
- Manrope

Typography pairing:
Serif = memory, legacy, emotion, storytelling.
Sans-serif = clarity, software, advisor trust, guided process.

BACKGROUND STYLE

Backgrounds should feel quiet, archival, premium, and warm.

Use:
- warm ivory paper-like backgrounds
- parchment beige surfaces
- deep charcoal background sections
- subtle grain/noise
- soft vignettes
- old-photo overlays
- paper texture
- shadowed product cards
- dark transparent overlays behind text
- subtle torn/deckled paper edges only when refined and minimal

Avoid:
- bright abstract gradients
- overused SaaS blobs
- neon glow
- harsh geometric patterns
- flat corporate blue backgrounds
- busy textures
- futuristic digital grids

The background should feel like a private family archive, not a generic startup landing page.

IMAGERY STYLE

Imagery should be emotional, restrained, real-feeling, and memory-led.

Use:
- vintage family photographs
- multi-generational family moments
- warm natural light
- slight film grain
- slightly imperfect framing
- soft blur or old-camera treatment
- heirloom objects such as letters, albums, books, cameras, photo frames, wax seals, handwritten notes
- quiet family scenes
- archival paper and photo collage compositions

Image treatment:
- warm contrast
- slight desaturation
- muted greens, browns, creams, and faded blues
- natural skin tones
- dark overlays when text is placed nearby
- crop like real family photos, not polished advertisements

Avoid:
- generic wealth-management stock photos
- luxury mansions or real estate
- office boardrooms
- overly perfect smiling family portraits
- glossy corporate photography
- cartoon families
- AI/futuristic visuals

UI MOCKUP STYLE

Create polished product visuals for a calm, guided family operating system. The UI should feel premium, organized, emotionally sensitive, and useful. It should not look like a raw screenshot or generic SaaS dashboard.

Use:
- rounded cards with soft shadows
- warm ivory surfaces
- calm dark dashboards
- timeline structures
- milestone cards
- Vision modules
- values and purpose sections
- family-member avatars and relationship chips
- legacy letter cards
- profile panels
- advisor dashboard views
- progress indicators
- soft dividers
- rust/red-brown badges
- small functional icons
- generous spacing and clear hierarchy

Use only short generic UI labels when needed:
Vision, Values, Purpose, Milestones, Roles, Family Members, Legacy Letter, Timeline, Stories, Notes, Advisor View, Progress, Review, Complete, Draft, Shared, Private.

Do not include:
- real names
- real family data
- long readable paragraphs
- brand names
- third-party logos
- watermarks
- raw screenshots
- crowded dashboards
- finance-heavy charts unless copy directly requires it

UI SCENE TYPES

The image prompt should choose the most relevant scene from the copy.

Family Vision Dashboard:
A calm dashboard showing Vision progress, values cards, family-member avatars, milestone status, and advisor notes. Use dark charcoal background with ivory cards and rust accent badges.

Milestone Timeline:
A vertical or horizontal timeline showing family milestones, legacy moments, role updates, letters, and completed steps. Add small archival photo cards or paper-note details.

Legacy Letter / Story Card:
A premium card showing a legacy letter, handwritten-style detail, date stamp, wax-seal accent, and soft paper texture. Keep text short and mostly unreadable.

Family Archive Collage:
A layered composition combining vintage family photos, paper textures, old letters, a product card, and small icon badges. Should feel like memory organized into a modern system.

Advisor Dashboard:
A structured advisor-facing dashboard with family profiles, Vision completion, upcoming conversation prompts, milestone progress, and review states. Serious, calm, and enterprise-ready.

Mobile / Responsive Product View:
One or two phone screens in front of a desktop dashboard. Show milestone timeline, family roles, values cards, or legacy notes. No hands unless specifically requested.

Guided Conversation Flow:
A step-by-step interface for family discussion prompts, purpose questions, values selection, and advisor-led notes. Use soft cards, progress steps, and warm editorial styling.

ICON / ILLUSTRATION STYLE

Icons should be minimal, warm, and slightly heritage-inspired.

Use:
- thin-line icons
- rounded corners
- clear silhouettes
- rust, charcoal, warm grey, ivory, or bronze colors
- small icon badges
- simple interface symbols

Good icon subjects:
- heart / connection
- letter / legacy note
- timeline / milestones
- family tree
- wax seal
- book / archive
- camera / memory
- compass / vision
- people / generations
- lock / privacy
- advisor / guidance

Illustration direction:
Use refined collage instead of flat cartoon illustration. Combine product UI, vintage family photography, paper textures, handwritten notes, wax-seal details, and small functional icons.

Avoid:
- cartoon characters
- 3D SaaS icons
- neon outlines
- cute family doodles
- generic finance icons like coins, vaults, or stock charts unless required by the copy
- complex futuristic illustrations

CREATIVE DIRECTION

Show transformation visually:

scattered family stories → organized family archive
unclear family purpose → shared Vision
one-off advisor conversations → guided repeatable process
old memories → living legacy
private family values → structured multi-generation alignment
family history → modern legacy operating system
manual legacy work → scalable advisor-led platform

Overall Creative Rule

Total Family should look like a living family archive supported by elegant software.

It is not a finance app.
It is not a legal planning tool.
It is not a generic SaaS dashboard.
It is not a luxury lifestyle brand.

It should feel warm, structured, timeless, quiet, premium, and deeply human.

LAYOUT RULE

Always keep a safe margin of at least 80px from all edges. Text should never touch the edges. Keep compositions spacious, calm, and uncluttered.
