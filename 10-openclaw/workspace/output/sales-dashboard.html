SOP Core

Non-negotiable rules

  • Every lead must be tracked in one system.
  • No lead without owner, stage, and next action.
  • No outreach without clear offer and ICP.
  • Follow-up discipline is mandatory.
  • Track lost reasons.
  • Use data to improve scripts and offers.
  • Keep outreach templates centralized.
  • Use ACS2 as default storage/work folder.

Cold Email

Email rules

  • Short subject line.
  • Relevant first line.
  • One problem, one promise, one CTA.
  • Under 120 words for first email.
Cold Calling

Call rules

  • Open fast.
  • State reason clearly.
  • Qualify quickly.
  • End with next step.
