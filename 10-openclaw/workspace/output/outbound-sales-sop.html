Outbound Sales SOP

Primary objective: generate revenue through cold email, cold calling, disciplined follow-up, and clear tracking.

100+

new leads added per day

30–50

cold calls per day

2–4

meetings booked per week target to start

System Rules

Non-negotiables

  • Every lead must be tracked in one system.
  • No lead without next action.
  • No call or email without offer clarity.
  • Follow-up matters more than first contact.
  • Daily reporting is mandatory.
Core Stack

Minimum working setup

  • Lead list: Google Sheets / CRM
  • Storage folder: ACS2
  • Pipeline: New → Researched → Contacted → Follow-up → Interested → Meeting Booked → Closed / Lost
  • Channels: cold email + cold calls
Daily Workflow

Execution flow

1. Lead sourcing: identify companies in target niches and add them to CRM.
2. Qualification: check if the company fits service potential, budget potential, and decision-maker availability.
3. Contact finding: collect owner/founder/marketing contact, email, phone, website, location.
4. Personalization: add one relevant note: weak website, poor SEO, weak ads, bad branding, no funnel, etc.
5. First outreach: send cold email and/or make first call with a short clear pitch.
6. Logging: record date, status, response, and next action immediately.
7. Follow-up: continue on a fixed cadence until reply, meeting, or clear rejection.
8. Handover: interested leads move to meeting/closing process.
Cold Email SOP

Email rules

  • Subject lines must be short and natural.
  • First line must prove relevance.
  • Pitch one problem, one promise, one CTA.
  • Keep under 120 words for first email.
  • CTA should ask for a short call, not a big commitment.

Suggested cadence

  • Day 1: first email
  • Day 3: follow-up 1
  • Day 6: follow-up 2
  • Day 10: final bump
Cold Calling SOP

Call rules

  • Open fast: who you are + why you called.
  • Do not over-explain in first 15 seconds.
  • Qualify interest quickly.
  • If not decision-maker, ask for the right person.
  • End every call with a defined next step.

Basic structure

  • Opening
  • Reason for call
  • Pain/opportunity hook
  • Question
  • CTA: book call / send details / callback time
Pipeline

Lead stages

Stage Meaning Required next action
New Lead added, not researched yet Research and qualify
Researched Data ready for outreach Send first email / make first call
Contacted Initial outreach done Schedule follow-up
Follow-up Waiting or continuing outreach Next touchpoint on date
Interested Lead showed intent Qualify and book meeting
Meeting Booked Call/demo scheduled Prep and close
Lost Rejected / not fit / dead Document reason
Daily Metrics

What to report every day

  • new leads added
  • emails sent
  • calls made
  • responses received
  • follow-ups completed
  • meetings booked
  • deals closed
  • top blockers
Weakness Checks

What usually breaks this system

  • No single owner for daily execution
  • Bad or generic offer
  • Low-quality leads
  • No follow-up discipline
  • Tracking outside the system
  • Too much manual work without templates
