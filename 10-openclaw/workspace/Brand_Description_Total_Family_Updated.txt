Brand Description

BRAND SUMMARY

Total Family is a legacy and family-alignment platform for families, advisors, wealth consultants, and enterprise firms. It helps families define a shared Vision, preserve stories, document values, clarify roles, track milestones, and strengthen connection across generations.

The brand is not primarily about wealth management, estate planning, or documents. The emotional core is legacy made practical: helping families turn memories, values, conversations, and long-term purpose into a guided, structured, and living system.

Total Family should feel thoughtful, human, timeless, warm, quietly premium, advisor-trusted, and emotionally intelligent. The brand lives between a family archive, a private home library, and a modern guided software platform.

Mission: Help families preserve what matters, align around shared values, and build a living legacy across generations.

Vision: Become the trusted platform for families and advisors who want legacy work to become structured, meaningful, repeatable, and deeply personal.

Tone: Warm, calm, reflective, premium, human, trustworthy, and clear. Avoid hype, aggressive sales language, cold SaaS language, and overly financial messaging.

Campaign / Messaging Anchor: “Doing Legacy Better.”

VISUAL STYLE

Total Family’s visual style combines warm family storytelling with quiet product proof.

Use full-bleed lifestyle photography, large editorial serif headlines, warm dark overlays, subtle film grain, soft vignettes, and floating product UI screenshots. The product should appear as an enabler inside the family moment, not as a detached tech object.

The design should feel like a living family archive supported by elegant software. It should be emotional but restrained, premium but not flashy, structured but not cold.

Use:
- warm family photography
- multi-generational home scenes
- editorial serif headlines
- italic emphasis for emotional words
- white or ivory text over darkened images
- subtle vertical divider lines
- frosted/translucent text panels when needed
- floating product UI overlays
- warm paper, wood, lamp-light, and archival textures
- calm, spacious layouts

Avoid:
- neon gradients
- cyber or futuristic visuals
- playful startup SaaS graphics
- cartoon families
- generic finance imagery
- legal-document-heavy visuals
- luxury real estate imagery
- shiny office boardrooms
- overly perfect stock family portraits
- abstract AI networks
- loud CTA buttons
- cluttered feature-heavy layouts

Color Usage Rules

Use warm photography, deep shadows, ivory text, and charcoal UI as the foundation.
Use rust and bronze sparingly as heritage accents.
Use beige, brown, and warm grey to create an archival, lived-in feeling.
Avoid bright blue, neon purple, electric gradients, and cold tech palettes.

CTA

Total Family creatives should not feel like hard-selling ads. CTAs should feel like calm invitations or reflective closing lines.

Preferred CTA style:
- text-based closing lines
- short product-proof statements
- rounded pill buttons only when a button is required
- dark charcoal, deep rust, or oxide red fill
- ivory or white text

Good CTA examples:
- Doing Legacy Better starts now.
- Start Your Family Vision
- Preserve What Matters
- Begin Legacy Work
- See How It Works
- Build a Living Legacy
- Unlock Advisor Demo
- Learn About Our Platform

Avoid:
- aggressive button CTAs
- neon buttons
- glow effects
- urgent sales language
- performance-marketing style banners

TYPOGRAPHY

Headlines should use an elegant high-contrast serif that feels literary, emotional, premium, and established.

Recommended headline font styles:
- Editorial New
- Canela
- Cormorant Garamond
- Playfair Display
- Libre Baskerville

Headline rules:
- use large editorial serif type
- use white or ivory text over darkened photography
- use generous line height
- use sentence case or title case
- avoid all-caps for main headlines
- use italic serif emphasis for emotional words such as legacy, families, values, together, unspoken, preserving, and what matters
- allow poetic line breaks
- keep copy short and declarative

Body and UI text should use a clean neutral sans-serif.

Recommended body/UI font styles:
- Inter
- Satoshi
- Neue Haas Grotesk
- Manrope

Typography pairing:
Serif = memory, legacy, emotion, storytelling.
Sans-serif = clarity, software, advisor trust, guided process.

Use:

IMAGERY STYLE

Imagery should be emotional, restrained, warm, and real-feeling.

Use:
- candid family moments at home
- parents, grandparents, children, and advisors in conversation
- families gathered around tables, laptops, books, albums, or living-room spaces
- warm kitchens, living rooms, desks, family rooms, and heirloom interiors
- lamps, wood furniture, framed photos, pianos, books, letters, and archival objects
- warm natural light or lamp light
- shallow depth of field
- slight film grain
- slightly imperfect framing
- muted greens, browns, creams, amber, and faded blues

Image treatment:
- add warm contrast
- slightly desaturate colors
- use dark overlays behind text
- keep faces natural, not overly retouched
- crop like real family memories, not polished ad campaigns

Do not use:
- generic wealth-management stock photos
- luxury mansions
- corporate boardroom scenes
- perfect smiling stock-family portraits
- glossy startup photography
- cartoon family illustration
- futuristic AI visuals

LAYOUT STYLE

The current creative direction uses square social-post style layouts.

Layout rules:
- full-bleed image as the main visual
- logo small in top-left or top-right depending on negative space
- large headline placed in upper third, center-left, or lower-left
- use dark overlay or gradient for readability
- use a thin vertical white divider line when it improves editorial framing
- product UI floats in the lower third, bottom corner, or side area
- UI should overlap the photo naturally
- lower-third frosted panels can hold product proof or secondary copy
- keep spacing generous and uncluttered

Do not overcrowd the design with many feature bullets, icons, or UI callouts.

UI MOCKUP STYLE

Create polished product visuals for a calm guided family operating system. The UI should feel premium, organized, emotionally sensitive, and useful.

The UI should be treated as a proof object, not the emotional hero. It should usually sit over photography as a floating screenshot or semi-transparent desktop window.

Use:
- dark left sidebar
- light main panel
- rounded cards
- soft shadow
- Family Vision interface
- family-member cards
- values cards
- milestone timelines
- shared vision modules
- family photo/video preview tiles
- advisor notes
- progress indicators
- profile avatars
- relationship chips
- muted orange/rust accents
- clean spacing

Use only short generic UI labels when needed:
Vision, Family Vision, Values, Purpose, Milestones, Roles, Family Members, Legacy Letter, Timeline, Stories, Notes, Advisor View, Progress, Review, Draft, Shared, Private, Chat with Kin AI.

Do not include:
- real names
- real family data
- long readable paragraphs
- third-party logos
- watermarks
- raw screenshots with poor composition
- cluttered dashboards
- finance-heavy charts unless copy directly requires it

UI SCENE TYPES

Choose the most relevant UI scene based on the copy.

Family Vision Dashboard:
A calm dashboard showing family Vision progress, values cards, family-member avatars, and advisor notes. Use dark sidebar, light cards, and subtle rust accents.

Milestone Timeline:
A vertical or horizontal timeline showing family milestones, legacy moments, role updates, letters, and completed steps. Pair with archival photo details.

Legacy Letter / Story Card:
A premium card showing a legacy letter, handwritten-style detail, date stamp, warm paper texture, and wax-seal accent. Keep text minimal and mostly unreadable.

Family Archive Collage:
A layered composition combining vintage family photos, paper textures, old letters, product UI, and small icon badges. Should feel like memory organized into a modern system.

Advisor Dashboard:
A structured advisor-facing dashboard with family profiles, Vision completion, upcoming conversation prompts, milestone progress, and review states. Serious, calm, and enterprise-ready.

Guided Conversation Flow:
A step-by-step interface for family discussion prompts, purpose questions, values selection, and advisor-led notes. Use soft cards, progress steps, and warm editorial styling.

Mobile / Responsive View:
One or two phone screens in front of a desktop dashboard. Show milestone timeline, values cards, family roles, or legacy notes. No hands unless specifically requested.

Icons should be minimal, warm, and functional. They should support the product or legacy theme without becoming playful.

Use:
- thin-line icons
- rounded corners
- simple silhouettes
- rust, charcoal, warm grey, ivory, or bronze colors
- small icon badges only when needed

Good icon subjects:
- heart / connection
- letter / legacy note
- timeline / milestones
- family tree
- wax seal
- book / archive
- camera / memory
- compass / vision
- people / generations
- lock / privacy
- advisor / guidance

Illustration direction:
Prefer refined collage over flat illustration. Combine product UI, family photography, paper texture, handwritten notes, subtle linework, and small functional icons.

Avoid:
- cartoon characters
- 3D SaaS icons
- neon outlines
- cute family doodles
- generic finance icons such as coins, vaults, or stock charts unless the copy specifically requires it
- complex futuristic illustrations

Use themes like:
- legacy beyond documents
- family alignment
- shared values
- unspoken conversations
- preserving what matters
- starting earlier, together
- common language across generations
- advisor-guided family conversations
- building, not just planning

Good copy style examples:

- Family alignment starts with shared language.

unspoken values → shared language
unclear purpose → family Vision
one-off advisor conversations → guided repeatable process
old memories → living legacy
family history → modern legacy operating system
manual legacy work → scalable advisor-led platform

Overall Creative Rule

Total Family should look like a living family archive supported by elegant software.

It is not a finance app.
It is not a legal planning tool.
It is not a generic SaaS dashboard.
It is not a luxury lifestyle brand.

It should feel warm, structured, timeless, quiet, premium, and deeply human.

LAYOUT SAFETY RULE

Always keep a safe margin of at least 80px from all edges. Text should never touch the edges. Keep compositions spacious, calm, and uncluttered.
