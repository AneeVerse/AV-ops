Truckstop 101
Truckstop was founded in 1995 in small-town Idaho as the Internet’s first load board—a better way for truck drivers to
find loads, connect to brokers, and run a more efficient business. Nearly 30 years later, we’re still delivering on what
we started: innovative solutions that help carriers, brokers, and shippers handle every phase of the freight lifecycle
with ease, speed, and confidence. With corporate headquarters in Boise, Idaho, today Truckstop employs nearly 700
people across the nation, all dedicated to supporting America’s freight professionals with the technology tools they
need to succeed in an ever-changing industry.

efforts and ensure consistency. We reserve the right to approve any messages released to your audience or the
public that mention our brand.

Truckstop and Competitors

Truckstop values your partnership. We understand that you may partner with competitive brands/ businesses that
offer similar products as ours and share mutual customers.

We kindly ask that if you present Truckstop to your audience via any form of communication (ex. email, blogs, etc.) or
promote our partnership,

DO NOT place Truckstop and a direct competitor side by side or mention brands together in the same subject line/
sentence.

If you have questions about how to present Truckstop to your audience, please contact the Truckstop Partner team.
